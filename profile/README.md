  The nest is where our tools are made. The sky is what they should give the people who use them: freedom.<br>
  That is why every app is named after a bird. Birds belong to no one and go where they please.

## Our goal

We build software that belongs to the people who use it: everyday tools you own instead of rent, that do what you expect of them and nothing else. A program should serve its user, not a business model. What we make should stay useful for as long as it is needed, even once we are no longer around.

## Where we stand

Much of today's software is built to hold on to people rather than to help them. You pay subscriptions for things you could buy once, create accounts where none is needed and give away data without noticing. Some programs get a little worse with every update, because they are meant to sell more. Suzora is a deliberate answer to that.

We are not a company and do not intend to become one. There are no investors, no growth targets and no exit, so there is no one who will one day need to make a return out of our users. We stand in the tradition of free software and on the shoulders of countless open-source projects, without which none of our tools would exist. What we build, we give back under the same free terms.

We are small and happy to stay that way. We do not build platforms but individual tools, each with one clear job, made by people who use them themselves. We use modern means to do it, AI tools included, and we say so openly. A human is still responsible for every line.

## What we hold to

**Freedom over control.** Anyone who uses our software should be able to understand it, change it and pass it on. No one should be able to take that freedom away again, not even us.

**Usefulness over profit.** We judge a tool by how much it helps, not by what it earns.

**Trust through openness.** Trust cannot be demanded, it has to be verifiable. That is why everything is open: the code, the decisions and the mistakes.

**Respect for people.** Our users' time, attention and data belong to them. We take only what the task really needs.

**Enough, not more.** A tool is good when it does its job reliably, not when it can do as much as possible. We grow in quality, not in scope.

**Honesty.** We only promise what we can keep, and we say openly what is not finished yet.

**Responsibility beyond our own users.** Our tools should harm no one, including the services and people they come into contact with.

## In practice

- **Free of charge, for good.** No subscriptions, no premium tiers, no paywalls, no ads. We sell neither software nor data. If you connect a paid third-party service, you pay that service, not us. Donations are voluntary and never needed: they unlock nothing, and anyone who gives nothing gets exactly the same.
- **Free as in freedom, for good.** Everything we publish is licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0 or any later version. Whoever passes our code on or runs it as a service must pass on the source under the same terms. We accept contributions without any transfer of rights, so no one can ever close the projects, not even us.
- **Private by default.** No metrics, no telemetry, no usage data. Our apps only connect where you send them. There is no Suzora account, no Suzora cloud and no server we run. Your data stays with you, and whatever an app stores, you can export and take with you.
- **Quality of life first.** Reliable, fast and pleasant to use comes before new features. One app, one job, done well.
- **Nothing pushy.** No nagging prompts, no dark patterns, no artificial urgency. And our tools are not built to get around protections or to abuse the services they work with.
- **Usable by as many people as possible.** That includes screen readers, keyboard-only use and other assistive tools. Since we do not rely on them ourselves, we are bound to miss things. If you run into a barrier, tell us. We take that seriously.

## Get involved

Every Suzora tool exists because we need it ourselves. Feedback, bug reports and ideas are welcome in each app's issue tracker, and if you would like to help build, there is room in the nest.
